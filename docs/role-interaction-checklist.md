# Role Interaction and Accountability Checklist

This template ensures proper coordination and clear accountability across all project roles during key project phases.

## Project Initiation Phase

### Pre-Project Setup
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Define initial vision and high-level requirements
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Establish project charter and stakeholder map
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Identify initial risks and constraints
- [ ] **All Roles** - Attend kickoff meeting and confirm role responsibilities

### Stakeholder Alignment
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Confirm stakeholder expectations and success criteria
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Establish communication cadence and escalation paths
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Document stakeholder-related risks and dependencies
- [ ] **Developers** - Understand technical constraints and feasibility factors
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Understand quality standards and acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Understand deployment requirements and environment constraints

## Planning Phase

### Requirements & Scope Definition
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Refine user stories and acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Developers** - Provide technical feasibility assessment and estimation
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Define test strategies and coverage requirements
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Identify scope-related risks and dependencies
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Coordinate and document all inputs

### Schedule & Resource Planning
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Create detailed project schedule with milestones
- [ ] **Developers** - Confirm capacity and identify resource constraints
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Allocate testing time and environment requirements
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Schedule deployment windows and environment prep
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Assess schedule risks and buffer recommendations

### Risk & Issue Planning
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Conduct risk assessment workshop with all roles
- [ ] **Developers** - Contribute technical risk insights
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Identify testing and quality risks
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Identify operational and deployment risks
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Contribute business and stakeholder risks
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Confirm risk ownership and escalation paths

## Execution Phase

### Weekly Team Syncs
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Facilitate status meeting and issue resolution
- [ ] **All Roles** - Report progress, blockers, and upcoming work
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Provide weekly risk update and escalations
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Confirm priorities and clarify requirements as needed

### Change Management
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Evaluate change requests and business impact
- [ ] **Developers** - Assess technical impact and effort
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Assess testing impact
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Assess deployment impact
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Assess risk implications
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Approve/deny based on schedule and resource impact

### Quality Checkpoints
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Confirm acceptance criteria understanding with Product Owner
- [ ] **Developers** - Address testing feedback and quality findings
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Validate work meets requirements
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Track quality metrics and defect trends

## Release & Deployment Phase

### Pre-Release Verification
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Complete test execution and sign-off on quality
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Prepare deployment plan and runbooks
- [ ] **Developers** - Address final issues and confirm release readiness
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Review deployment risks and rollback procedures
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Confirm feature readiness and beta plan
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Coordinate release communication

### Deployment Execution
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Execute deployment and monitor for issues
- [ ] **Developers** - Available for troubleshooting and hotfixes
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Perform smoke testing and validation
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Communicate status to stakeholders
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Monitor for and escalate deployment risks

## Post-Release Phase

### Deployment Validation
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Confirm production stability
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Execute post-deployment testing
- [ ] **Developers** - Monitor logs and metrics for issues
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Confirm business metrics and user feedback
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Gather final status and lessons learned

### Retrospective & Closure
- [ ] **Project Manager** - Facilitate retrospective with all roles
- [ ] **Risk Manager** - Document risk management effectiveness
- [ ] **QA Analyst** - Report final quality metrics and testing insights
- [ ] **Deployment Engineer** - Document deployment lessons and improvements
- [ ] **Developers** - Share technical insights and improvement suggestions
- [ ] **Product Owner** - Confirm stakeholder satisfaction and outcomes

---

## Notes
- Use this checklist during project planning to identify missing activities or role engagement
- Customize based on project complexity and organizational standards
- Track completion to ensure accountability and alignment across all roles
- Use findings in retrospectives to improve process maturity
